# Purpose

Create a LinkedIn-optimized post that drives engagement and demonstrates engineering skill.

## Structure

1. **Hook** — First line grabs attention (technical insight, surprising outcome, specific number).
2. **Story** — 2-3 short paragraphs explaining what was built or solved.
3. **Lesson learned** — One concrete takeaway the reader can apply.
4. **Question** — End with a question to drive comments.

## Rules

- Maximum 300 words.
- No hashtag spam — maximum 5 relevant hashtags at the end.
- Write for engineers and technical managers.
- Be specific: name technologies, mention real outcomes.
- Never oversell. Let the work speak.

## Tone

Confident but not arrogant. Helpful, not promotional.
