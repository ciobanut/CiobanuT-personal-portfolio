# 15 Apr 2026

Linked behavora predictions and banner impressions to visitor journeys, and renamed `impressed_at` to `delivered_at`.

## Projects

### behavora (journey-predictor-api)

**Journey linking.** Linked predictions and banner impressions to visitor journeys (+195/−60) so every decision and impression is traceable to the journey that produced it.

**Impression model.** Updated the `BannerImpression` model and renamed `impressed_at` to `delivered_at` (+33/−40), aligning the field with the delivery event it records.

### All changes

- Linked predictions/impressions to visitor journeys
- Renamed impressed_at → delivered_at

## Lessons

The `impressed_at` → `delivered_at` rename is honest naming: "impression" implies the visitor saw it, "delivered" only records the delivery attempt — a distinction that matters for metrics.
