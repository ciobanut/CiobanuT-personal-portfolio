# 18 May 2026

Made behavora's onboarding steps JSON-driven, added target highlighting with popovers, and drafted the Onboarding Checklist & Activation System.

## Projects

### behavora (journey-predictor-api)

**JSON-driven steps.** Converted onboarding steps to a JSON-driven definition (+523/−145) instead of hard-coded steps in the component. Replaced `#[Computed]` properties with regular public `$steps` in the onboarding component (+30/−18).

**Highlighting.** Fixed first-step highlight type, highlighted the form on the first step, and made `driver_target` highlight with a popover (+56/−2). Added the `#create_or_update_website` anchor to the first step.

**Activation system.** Drafted the Onboarding Checklist & Activation System (+2,512/−631) — the larger activation flow this checklist supports. Also fixed a draft modal-not-open issue (+789/−7).

### All changes

- Made onboarding steps JSON-driven
- Replaced Computed properties with public $steps
- Fixed step highlighting (form, driver_target popover)
- Drafted Onboarding Checklist & Activation System
- Fixed draft modal not opening

## Lessons

JSON-driven onboarding makes steps data instead of code — adding a step is editing config, not touching component logic.
