# 2 Dec 2025

Limited post excerpts, ensured slug uniqueness, and displayed the latest published posts on the landing site.

## Projects

### landing

**Excerpts.** Limited post excerpts on the home page to 20 words (+5) and displayed the "more posts" button only when there are more posts (+6).

**Slugs.** Refactored slug generation in the `Post` model to ensure uniqueness (+11) and added slug attribute handling (+12).

**Posts.** Displayed the latest published posts on the home and post-show pages (+94).

### All changes

- Limited excerpts to 20 words
- Ensured unique slug generation
- Displayed latest published posts

## Lessons

Slug uniqueness is a silent correctness bug — duplicates break routes; generating unique slugs at save time prevents it.
