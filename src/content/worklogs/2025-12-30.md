# 30 Dec 2025

Implemented dynamic sitemap generation and www-to-non-www redirects on the landing site.

## Projects

### landing

**Sitemap.** Implemented sitemap generation with dynamic post URLs (+70/−35), filtering posts by `PUBLISHED` status (+1) so unpublished posts never appear.

**Redirects.** Added a redirect from www to non-www in `.htaccess` (+4), then commented it out and removed it after testing.

### All changes

- Implemented dynamic sitemap with post URLs
- Filtered sitemap posts by published status
- Added (then removed) www→non-www redirect

## Lessons

A sitemap is only trustworthy if it reflects publication state — filtering by status at generation time prevents unpublished content from being crawled.
