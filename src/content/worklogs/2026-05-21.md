# 21 May 2026

Extracted Bloop-landing's settings, support and pages tabs into dedicated surfaces, and added the travelpayouts localrent widget to MyPrivateOrder ports pages.

## Projects

### Bloop-landing

**UX extraction.** Extracted all website settings into a separate page for better UX (+773/−411), extracted the support page out of website settings into the landing-pages menu (+213/−147), and extracted the tabs with pages from the website-settings Filament resource into a separate surface (+928/−292). Added a `navigationSort` property for consistent page ordering, made the message field required, and applied Copilot suggestions.

### myprivateorder.com

**Localrent widget.** Added the travelpayouts `localrent` widget to ports pages (+613/−1,713).

### All changes

- Extracted website settings, support and pages into separate pages
- Added navigationSort ordering
- Added travelpayouts localrent widget to ports pages

## Lessons

A Filament resource that grew into many tabs should be split before it becomes a mega-form — each extraction keeps one concern per page.
