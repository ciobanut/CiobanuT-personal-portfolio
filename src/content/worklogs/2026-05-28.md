# 28 May 2026

Divided behavora's banner settings and the Eligibility & Targeting page into separate, focused pages, and fixed Bloop-API chat response shape.

## Projects

### behavora (journey-predictor-api)

**Settings split.** Divided banner settings into separate pages (+825/−277) — each concern (targeting, display, dismissal…) gets its own page instead of one long form.

**Eligibility & Targeting.** Split the combined "Eligibility & Targeting" page into separate pages and improved their UX (+306/−282).

### Bloop-API

**Chat schema.** Added the missing top-level `data` wrapper to the 200-response schema of the chat endpoint (+121/−80), updated `test_advertiser_can_view_chat` to verify `organization_id`, and returned the influencer ID associated with the chat in advertiser chat responses.

### All changes

- Split banner settings into separate pages
- Split Eligibility & Targeting into separate pages with UX improvements
- Added top-level data wrapper to chat 200 schema
- Added influencer ID to advertiser chat response

## Lessons

One-settings-per-page beats mega-forms: each page becomes independently navigable and testable, and the +825 is repaid in maintainability.
