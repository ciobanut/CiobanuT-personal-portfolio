# 8 Jul 2026

Added six preconfigured scoring preset templates (Hot Buyers, Engaged Shoppers, etc.) to behavora, implemented real click/impression tracking for banners, and tidied prediction options.

## Projects

### behavora (journey-predictor-api)

**Scoring preset templates.** Added 6 cards with preconfigured templates — Hot Buyers, Engaged Shoppers and similar (+546/−119). Replaced emoji in scoring rule presets with heroicons for a cleaner look, and fixed the "UX & Behavior" options to show all choices with defaults.

**Click/impression tracking.** Added click and impression tracking methods to banners (+213), fixing the banner impressions/clicks counters to reflect real events.

**Prediction options.** Disabled the AI-powered prediction strategy option and improved the Scoring Rules title.

**Shopify widget.** Added `defer` to the widget script for Shopify compatibility, added a "Preview Banner" option to the banner submenu, and hid the unused Banner Animations menu link.

### All changes

- Added 6 preconfigured scoring preset templates
- Implemented banner click/impression tracking methods
- Replaced preset emoji with heroicons
- Showed all UX & Behavior options with defaults
- Disabled AI prediction strategy option
- Added `defer` to Shopify widget script; added Preview Banner submenu item

## Lessons

Templates turn a blank scoring-rules page into an onboarding path — six archetypes (hot buyers, engaged shoppers) give users a starting point instead of an empty form.

Click/impression counters are only trustworthy once they read real events; the tracking methods commit makes the dashboard numbers honest.
