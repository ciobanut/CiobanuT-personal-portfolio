# 6 Jul 2026

Implemented behavora's banner display rules: timing and frequency controls (+853), the display trigger including exit-intent, and the exit-intent banner fix.

## Projects

### behavora (journey-predictor-api)

**Display timing & frequency.** Implemented display timing and frequency rules for banners (+853/−32) — when a banner may show and how often, configurable per banner.

**Display trigger.** Implemented the banner display trigger (+537/−221): the condition that decides whether a banner is eligible to display at all.

**Exit-intent fix.** Fixed exit-intent banners not showing (+205/−8) — the trigger path wasn't resolving the exit-intent condition correctly.

### All changes

- Implemented display timing and frequency rules
- Implemented banner display trigger
- Fixed exit-intent banners not showing

## Lessons

Display logic is layered: a banner must be _eligible_ (trigger), _timed_ (when), and _frequency-capped_ (how often). Building the three as separate evaluators keeps each rule testable on its own.
